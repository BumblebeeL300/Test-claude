# Greenhouse notes

## Choosing a site

A greenhouse needs six hours of direct sun in midwinter, shelter from the prevailing wind, and
level, well-drained ground. Trees to the south cast the longest shadows in winter, so they matter
more than trees to the north. Access to water close by saves hours of carrying during the summer.

## Ventilation and temperature

Roof vents let hot air escape, and low side vents pull in cool air, which creates a gentle draught
through the house. On sunny spring days the temperature can rise by ten degrees within half an hour,
so vents should open automatically. Automatic openers use a wax cylinder that expands with heat and
need no electricity.

## Watering

Water in the morning so that leaves dry before night, because damp leaves in a cold greenhouse
invite mould. Seedlings need small amounts often, while established tomatoes prefer a deep soak
every few days. A layer of mulch on the soil slows evaporation and keeps the roots evenly moist.
