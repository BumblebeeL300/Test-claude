# Incident handbook

## Severity levels

Every incident gets a severity when it is declared. Severity 1 means customers cannot use the core
product at all, severity 2 means a major feature is degraded for many customers, and severity 3
covers everything that can wait for business hours. The incident commander may change the severity
at any time, and every change is written to the incident channel.

## Roles during an incident

The incident commander coordinates the response and makes the final call on risky actions. The
operations lead runs the commands and changes, and the communications lead posts a status update
every thirty minutes. Nobody fills more than one role, so that the commander always has time to think
about the next decision instead of typing commands.

## After the incident

A blameless review is held within five working days. The review asks what made the failure possible
and why the alarm was late, not who pressed the wrong button. Each review ends with a short list of
follow-up tasks, and every task has one owner and a due date.
